# Dark Matter Navigator

Impressive figures, always with one eye to the future and one to the void. They plug into the probability mainframe, parsing uncertainty from sensors, galaxy fluctations and the echos of the Quantum Cascade.
This means taking many readings and comparing, which is why there are so many Navigators. For them, there is never a single answer.

## Starting Move

### Speak in One Voice

This replaces Make A Statement.

When you speak or warn on behalf of the Navigator Collective. Roll 2d6+ Expressive.

On a 10+ your statement is heard, and the reaction you seek happens urgently, widespreadly or magnificently.  
On a 7-9 your statement is heard, and minds are moved.
On a 6- your attempt to disrupt the known conventions fails and upsets powerful people.
